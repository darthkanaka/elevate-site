The studio

Small business mentality

Elevate Media is built on relationships and trust. You get the care, attention and thoughtfulness you deserve for your marketing goals. As creatives, our biggest reward is watching our clients succeed.

Over a decade behind the camera, years of it as director of photography on Hi*Sessions, Hawaii's biggest source of music video content. That's where the Hawaiian Airlines work started and it kept going from there. Web content, campaigns with their credit card team, an image licensing deal, pieces that played on in-flight entertainment, shoots for their HR department.

The rest has gone to car brands, resorts, schools and the people who run professional surfing. Tiffen, who make the filters a lot of shooters work through, put us on their Flight Team. And a video we shot for Civil Beat ran with a story that went on to win awards.
